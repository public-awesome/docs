# Superstars Program

On August 2025, Stargaze introduced a new feature called Superstars Program.\
The main purpose of the Program is to incentivize staking and volume, while giving different perks depending on how much each user has staked.\
\
To ge to a certain tier, you need to mint the Superstars NFT on your profile, and stake a minimum amount of STARS. The current discounts are:\
\
Tier 0: 5K STARS: 5% fee reduction

Tier 1: 100K STARS: 12.5% fee reduction

Tier 2: 500K STARS: 25% fee reduction

Tier 3: 1.25M STARS: 37.5% fee reduction

Tier 4: 2.5M STARS: 50% fee reduction\
\
Every time you perform an action on the Marketplace, your tier will update accordingly to how much you have staked. If you have staked outside of the Stargaze website and have not done any Marketplace transactions, you can update your tier manually going to your profile.\
\
To get started, go to [https://stargaze.zone/superstar](https://stargaze.zone/superstar) and mint your Superstar NFT!\
\
Perks for creators will be coming out soon, tied to the Superstars program.
